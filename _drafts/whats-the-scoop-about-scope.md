## What the point of scope?

A couple of concepts I struggled with in my early days was scope and somewhat in a similar manner readonly. Why make things more complicated and and stuff that stops you doing what you want to do?

With time and wisdom and from seeing a lot of code which did not see the point either. Scope is both a guardrail and a nudge to convey your intent to future developers for what is and isn't the right way to do things. Have you ever found a library makes it unreasonably difficult to do something? Odds are that is a nudge to drive you towards the right way.
